# Mobile App Quality Rubric

Score each category from 0 to 3.

## Categories

- `Fit`: matches the requested mobile use case.
- `Completeness`: implements core interactions.
- `Touch UX`: controls are reachable, tappable, and clear.
- `Navigation`: screens are predictable and not overbuilt.
- `State Coverage`: empty, active, complete, and error states are covered.
- `Visual Polish`: feels like a real mobile utility prototype.
- `Mobile Ergonomics`: comfortable touch targets, readable density, no overflow.
- `Code Quality`: clear components, typed data, simple state.
- `Local-First`: no backend, accounts, push, or integrations unless requested.

## Passing Bar

A generated prototype should average at least 2.5 and have no score below 2.
