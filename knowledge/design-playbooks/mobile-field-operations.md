# Mobile Field Operations Design Playbook

Use for field service, maintenance requests, inspections, and technician workflows.

## Priorities

- Make status, location/context, priority, and next action visible first.
- Support offline-looking local state, queued actions, and completion summaries.
- Keep checklists thumb-friendly with stable row heights.
- Use clear severity/status language and accessible badges.

## Avoid

- Desktop table layouts on mobile.
- Hidden primary actions below long notes.
- Camera, map, signature, or upload integrations unless explicitly requested.
