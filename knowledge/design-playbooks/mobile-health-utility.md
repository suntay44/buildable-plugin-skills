# Mobile Health Utility Design Playbook

Use for medication reminders, wellness journals, check-ins, and health-adjacent utilities.

## Priorities

- Use calm, legible, non-alarming copy.
- Keep the next safe action obvious and reachable.
- Show missed, empty, completed, and error states without shame language.
- Make touch targets large and state labels text-based, not color-only.

## Avoid

- Medical advice, diagnosis, dosage recommendations, or emergency claims.
- Dark patterns around streaks, missed doses, or mood history.
- Tiny controls, hidden edit actions, or notification assumptions.
