# Reporting Workspace Design Playbook

Use for dashboards, report builders, analytics workspaces, and saved-view tools.

## Priorities

- Keep filters, date range, saved views, and preview blocks in one understandable workspace.
- Pair every chart/table with context, labels, and the active filter state.
- Make empty reports useful: offer the first chart/table block and sample data.
- Preserve scanability before visual flourish.

## Avoid

- Charts without labels, units, or data source context.
- Decorative metrics that cannot be filtered or compared.
- Export buttons that imply a real integration when the prototype is mock/local.
