# Commerce Design Playbook

Use for storefronts, carts, product catalogs, ecommerce admin, and purchase-intent flows.

## Priorities

- Make product identity, price, availability, and primary action visible without hunting.
- Keep filters, sort, and active-filter chips near the catalog.
- Design cart and empty-cart states as real states, not afterthoughts.
- Use trust cues with concrete copy, not generic badges.

## Avoid

- Fake checkout or payment flows unless explicitly requested.
- Product cards with decorative images but no useful metadata.
- Hiding price, quantity, availability, or cart state.
