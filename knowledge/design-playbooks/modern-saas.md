# Modern SaaS Design Playbook

## Tone

Operational, polished, and restrained.

## Layout

- dense but readable information hierarchy
- metric summaries near the top
- tables and charts designed for scanning
- clear navigation when multiple views exist

## Common Mistakes

- marketing-style hero sections inside the product UI
- decorative gradients replacing useful information
- charts without labels or context

## Quality Bar

- metric cards should answer what changed and whether it matters
- filters should visibly affect charts or tables
- the first screen should feel operational, not promotional
- color should distinguish status and trend, not just brand mood
