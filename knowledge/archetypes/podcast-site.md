# Podcast Site Archetype

## Purpose

Present podcast episodes, show notes, topics, and featured content.

## Default Screens

- `episodes`: episode list and featured episode.
- `episode detail`: show notes, metadata, and related episodes.

## Entities

- `Episode`: title, guest, topic, duration, publishedAt, notes.

## Required Interactions

- Browse episodes, filter topics, open details, and keep mobile layout readable.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Audio hosting, RSS publishing, paid feeds, or analytics.
