# CRM Archetype

## Purpose

A CRM helps users track leads, contacts, companies, pipeline stages, next actions, and deal value.

## Default Screens

- pipeline overview
- lead/contact list
- lead detail or side panel

## Default Entities

- Lead
- Company
- Activity
- Deal

## Required Interactions

- create and edit leads
- move leads between stages
- filter by stage or owner
- search contacts or companies
- record a next action

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- email sending
- external CRM sync
- team permissions
- billing
- hosted database
