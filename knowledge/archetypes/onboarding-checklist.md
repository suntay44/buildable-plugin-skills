# Onboarding Checklist Archetype

## Purpose

Guide customers, employees, or users through setup steps.

## Default Screens

- `checklist`: progress summary and grouped steps.
- `step detail`: task instructions, owner, and due date.

## Entities

- `OnboardingStep`: title, group, status, owner, dueDate, notes.

## Required Interactions

- Complete/reopen steps, filter by status, and show progress clearly.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- User provisioning, HRIS sync, email automation, or team permissions.
