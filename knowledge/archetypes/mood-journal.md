# Mood Journal Archetype

Build a calm mobile wellness journal for daily mood check-ins, short reflections, and local history.

## Core Shape

- Today screen with mood selection, reflection prompt, tags, and save state.
- History screen grouped by date with mood trend summary.
- Empty history state that encourages a first check-in without guilt.

## Local-First Rules

- Keep entries local/mock by default.
- Avoid medical claims, diagnosis language, crisis handling claims, cloud sync, accounts, or notifications unless explicitly requested.
- Use supportive copy, not clinical or judgmental copy.
