# Asset Tracker Archetype

## Purpose

Track equipment, assignments, status, and maintenance notes.

## Default Screens

- `assets`: asset list with status filters.
- `asset detail`: assignment, condition, and maintenance history.

## Entities

- `Asset`: name, type, status, owner, location, maintenanceDate.

## Required Interactions

- Filter assets, update status, inspect assignments, and show empty states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Device telemetry, procurement systems, GPS tracking, or role-based permissions.
