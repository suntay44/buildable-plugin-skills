# Restaurant Archetype

## Purpose

A restaurant site presents menu, hours, location, featured items, and reservation/contact actions.

## Default Screens

- home
- menu

## Default Entities

- MenuItem
- MenuSection

## Required Interactions

- browse menu sections
- highlight featured items
- show hours/location
- reservation or contact CTA

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- online ordering
- payments
- delivery integrations
