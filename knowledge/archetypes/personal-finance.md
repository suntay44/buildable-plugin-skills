# Personal Finance Archetype

## Purpose

Show spending, account summaries, and budget health using local sample data.

## Default Screens

- `overview`: financial summary cards, cash flow, and category breakdown.
- `accounts`: account list and recent transactions.

## Entities

- `Account`: name, type, balance, trend.
- `Transaction`: merchant, amount, category, date.

## Required Interactions

- Filter transactions, inspect categories, and show empty account or transaction states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Bank connections, financial advice, payments, or real account storage.
