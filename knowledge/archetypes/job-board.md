# Job Board Archetype

## Purpose

A job board helps users browse roles, filter opportunities, inspect details, and apply or express interest.

## Default Screens

- jobs
- job detail

## Default Entities

- Job
- Company

## Required Interactions

- job list
- search and filters
- detail view
- apply CTA
- empty state

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- accounts
- applicant tracking backend
- email delivery
