# Landing Page Archetype

## Purpose

A landing page presents a focused offer, product, service, or waitlist and drives one primary action.

## Default Screens

- home

## Default Entities

- Section
- Testimonial
- PricingOption

## Required Interactions

- primary CTA
- responsive navigation
- feature/value sections
- social proof or proof points
- final CTA

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- auth
- billing
- backend CMS
- deployment
