# Volunteer Management Archetype

## Purpose

Coordinate volunteers, shift availability, and sign-up states.

## Default Screens

- `volunteers`: volunteer list and status filters.
- `shift detail`: shift needs, assigned volunteers, and notes.

## Entities

- `Volunteer`: name, skills, availability, status.
- `Shift`: date, role, capacity, assignedCount.

## Required Interactions

- Filter volunteers, assign shifts, inspect availability, and show empty states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Email reminders, background checks, accounts, or external scheduling tools.
