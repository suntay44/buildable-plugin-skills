# Video Library Archetype

## Purpose

Browse videos, categories, watch states, and detail content.

## Default Screens

- `library`: video grid/list and category filters.
- `video detail`: metadata, description, and watch state.

## Entities

- `Video`: title, category, duration, thumbnail, status, description.

## Required Interactions

- Filter videos, open details, mark watch state, and show empty states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Streaming infrastructure, uploads, payments, accounts, or DRM.
