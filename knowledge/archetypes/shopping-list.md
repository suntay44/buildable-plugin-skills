# Shopping List Archetype

Build a fast mobile grocery or shopping list with category grouping, quantity notes, and store-mode completion.

## Core Shape

- List screen with quick add, item groups, checked state, and filters.
- Store mode that keeps remaining items prominent and completed items collapsed.
- Item detail/edit state for quantity, category, notes, and priority.

## Local-First Rules

- Use local list seed data.
- Do not add grocery APIs, price comparison, barcode services, accounts, or sync unless explicitly requested.
