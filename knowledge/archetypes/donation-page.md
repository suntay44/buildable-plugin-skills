# Donation Page Archetype

## Purpose

Present a nonprofit campaign or fundraiser with impact context.

## Default Screens

- `campaign`: campaign hero, impact sections, progress summary, and CTA.

## Entities

- `Campaign`: title, goal, raised, impact, deadline, updates.

## Required Interactions

- Show progress, impact details, and donation intent without processing payments.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Payment processing, donor accounts, tax receipts, or nonprofit CRM sync.
