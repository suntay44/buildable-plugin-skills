# Newsletter Archetype

## Purpose

Show newsletter issues, archive browsing, and subscription intent.

## Default Screens

- `archive`: issue list, featured issue, and topic filters.
- `issue detail`: issue content and related issues.

## Entities

- `NewsletterIssue`: title, topic, excerpt, publishedAt, body.

## Required Interactions

- Browse issues, filter by topic, view details, and show empty archive states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Email sending, subscriber storage, paid subscriptions, or analytics.
