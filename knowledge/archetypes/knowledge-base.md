# Knowledge Base Archetype

## Purpose

Create a searchable support article and FAQ experience.

## Default Screens

- `article index`: categories, featured articles, and search.
- `article detail`: article content and related links.

## Entities

- `Article`: title, category, summary, body, updatedAt.

## Required Interactions

- Search articles, filter by category, open details, and show empty search states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- CMS backend, ticketing integrations, analytics, or user accounts.
