# Hiring Tracker Archetype

## Purpose

Track candidates through hiring stages and interview workflows.

## Default Screens

- `candidate pipeline`: grouped candidates by stage.
- `candidate detail`: profile, notes, interviews, and score.

## Entities

- `Candidate`: name, role, stage, score, nextInterview, notes.

## Required Interactions

- Move candidates between stages, filter by role/status, and show empty pipeline states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Email automation, background checks, job posting integrations, or accounts.
