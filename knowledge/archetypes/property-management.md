# Property Management Archetype

## Purpose

Manage properties, tenants, lease status, and maintenance requests.

## Default Screens

- `properties`: portfolio list and occupancy summary.
- `tenant detail`: tenant, lease, rent, and maintenance context.

## Entities

- `Property`: address, type, occupancy, rent, status.
- `Tenant`: name, unit, leaseEnd, balance, status.

## Required Interactions

- Filter properties, inspect tenants, and track maintenance request status.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Rent payments, credit checks, document signing, or tenant accounts.
