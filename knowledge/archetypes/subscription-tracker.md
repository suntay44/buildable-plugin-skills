# Subscription Tracker Archetype

## Purpose

Track recurring subscriptions, renewal dates, and monthly costs.

## Default Screens

- `subscriptions`: list grouped by status or renewal timing.
- `renewal detail`: service details, cost, and renewal notes.

## Entities

- `Subscription`: service, cost, billing period, renewal date, status, category.

## Required Interactions

- Add or edit subscriptions, filter by category, and summarize recurring cost.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Payment collection, cancellation automation, bank sync, or email reminders.
