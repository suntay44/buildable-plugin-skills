# Clinic Intake Archetype

## Purpose

Prototype a local patient or appointment intake flow without storing real medical data.

## Default Screens

- `intake form`: multi-section form with validation.
- `submission summary`: review state and confirmation.

## Entities

- `IntakeSubmission`: patientName, reason, appointmentType, status, submittedAt.

## Required Interactions

- Validate required fields, review before submission, and show confirmation state.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Real PHI storage, medical advice, insurance checks, accounts, or backend systems.
