# Product Feedback Archetype

## Purpose

Collect and triage feature requests, ideas, and user feedback.

## Default Screens

- `feedback`: list or board of requests.
- `request detail`: description, status, priority, and votes.

## Entities

- `FeedbackItem`: title, description, status, priority, votes, category.

## Required Interactions

- Filter feedback, update status, inspect detail, and show empty states.

## Do Not Add Unless Requested

- Public voting auth, notifications, integrations, or roadmap publishing.
