# Ecommerce Storefront Planned Template

Use this pack when a prompt asks for an online shop, storefront, catalog, cart, or product page and no runnable storefront starter exists yet.

## Build Shape

- Catalog page with search, category filters, active-filter chips, product cards, and results count.
- Product detail surface with description, price, availability, variants/options, and add-to-cart action.
- Cart surface with quantities, subtotal, empty state, and checkout handoff placeholder.

## Guardrails

- Mock all catalog, cart, and checkout data locally.
- Do not add payments, shipping APIs, accounts, inventory sync, analytics, or hosted checkout unless explicitly requested.
