# Block: Mobile List With Filters

Use when the primary mobile workflow starts from a list of local records.

Fit:

- tasks, habits, bookings, expenses, trips, jobs, subscriptions
- search and lightweight filtering on small screens

Bind to the app spec:

- Use sticky or near-top filters only when they do not crowd the first screen.
- Prefer segmented controls or horizontal chips for 2-5 common states.
- Keep touch targets at least 44px.
- Show the most important status, time, or amount in the list item.
- Include empty, filtered-empty, loading, and offline/local states.

Avoid:

- desktop-style table layouts
- too many filters on the first screen
- tiny row actions that are hard to tap
